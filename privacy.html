Privacy

How we handle your enquiry.

When you submit the project enquiry form, Formal Electrical and Maintenance (Pty) Ltd receives the name and email address you provide, along with any optional contact number, location, service selection and message.

How the information is used

We use these details to review and respond to the enquiry. Enquiries are stored by the website operator so authorised team members can follow up. Do not include passwords, payment card details, identity numbers or other sensitive information in the form.

Retention and access

Enquiry records are available only to authorised site administrators. They should be removed when they are no longer needed for follow-up or record keeping. You may ask about information you submitted by contacting the company through a verified company contact channel.

Website operation

The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address and browser details to operate and protect the website. This website does not use advertising trackers.
